ExtensionDesk

Planting advice for small farmers, prepared by AI and signed off by a real farm officer.

In every Nigerian Local Government Area there is a small office of agricultural extension officers: government farm advisers who tell farmers when to plant, when to wait and which pests to watch for. One office can have two officers for thousands of farmers. Checking the rain, the pest reports and each village's crops takes them about 15 minutes per village, so most villages get late advice or none.

ExtensionDesk sits at the officer's desk and does that homework. It writes the SMS. The officer reads it, signs it with their name and decides whether it goes out.

Code: github.com/Marvel-Asika/extensiondesk

How it works, in four steps

The same four steps happen for every village. The computer does the slow checking. The officer keeps the final say.

Data
Gather the factsSatellite rainfall for the village, pest warnings, the list of farming families and the officer's notes.
AI assistant (runs offline)
Write a draft SMSIn simple words, with the source of every fact listed underneath.
The officer
A person decidesEverything pauses. The officer types their name and approves, edits or rejects each draft.
Outbox
Only approved SMS go outEvery step is recorded: what was checked, when, and who approved it.

The rain it reads

Real daily rainfall from satellites (CHIRPS) for two parts of Ikara LGA, May and June 2025. Plain code, not the AI, works out when the rains truly started and whether any days are missing.

What it will not let the AI do

A checker reads every draft before the officer sees it. Each grey line is the real message it gives when it sends a draft back.

Advice with no sourceReason 'Rains are good' has no source. Every claim needs a source ID.
Made-up sourcesSource 'NIMET:2025-06' was never returned by a tool.
Advice that contradicts the rainfall'plant' contradicts the rainfall rule verdict 'insufficient_data'.
Planting outside the crop window2025-06-20 is outside the planting window for cowpea (07-01 to 08-10).
Sending before the officer says yesDRAFT-0001 is PENDING_OFFICER. Only drafts approved by a named officer can be released.
The computer signing for the officerA real officer name is required to record a decision.
